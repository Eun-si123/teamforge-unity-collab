03 / Selected workflows

Shared context.
Deliberate boundaries.

TeamForge complements Git and Unity Version Control. It shares supported live work; it does not synchronize every part of a Unity project.

Capability status and limitations →
01 Presence & awareness
See connected collaborators, selections and Editor context.
02 Transform & ownership
Share supported Transform edits with server-authoritative locking. Still stabilizing.
03 Same-Scene Hierarchy
Create, delete, rename, reparent and reorder within the supported subset. Still stabilizing.
04 Project bootstrap
Join through a signed invite and verified direct project transfer. Internet reachability needs configuration.
04 / Between commits

Keep your history.
Share the live work.

Source control records the project. TeamForge connects the people editing it, while the work is happening.
